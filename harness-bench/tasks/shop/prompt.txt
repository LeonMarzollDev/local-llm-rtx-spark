In the shop package: rename calc_total to compute_total everywhere (no alias, no leftover uses of the old name, also not in tests). Then give compute_total a keyword-only parameter tax_rate (a float, default 0.0): the tax is computed on the total after the discount and added to it, everything rounded to cents. Cart gets a tax_rate attribute (default 0.0) that Cart.total() and the receipt use. When tax_rate is not 0, the last line of the receipt is replaced by three lines: "net: 14.40 EUR", "tax 19%: 2.74 EUR", "total: 17.14 EUR" (example for net 14.40 and tax_rate 0.19; the percentage without decimals). With tax_rate 0 the receipt stays as it is. Update the tests in test_shop.py accordingly and make sure they pass (python3 -m unittest -v).
